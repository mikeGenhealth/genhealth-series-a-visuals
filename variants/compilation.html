Language of the work

Round 2 · product metaphor
Mood
Fluent in the language of the work.
Typography-forward. Healthcare vocabulary — codes, diagnoses, policy lines, chart narrative — rendered like specimens, then parsed live on screen. The story: operational fluency as the basis for trust.

05 · Warm steel blue

Machine & human

Round 2 · product metaphor
Mood
AI coworkers, not tools.
Employee roster in the hero, department cards, an onboarding checklist that treats agents like new hires, and Slack-style activity logs that replace the workflow carousel. The most humanizing of the variants.

06 · Superpower teal

Precision & instrument

Round 2 · product metaphor
Mood
A laboratory instrument.
IBM Plex and grid paper. Instrument panel with live oscilloscope. Spec-sheet stats. Sectioned § typography throughout. The story: engineered for this, measured, legible, at home with technical buyers.

07 · Signal amber

Dispatch board

Round 3 · voice & narrative
Mood
Healthcare ops, live.
The nerve-center interpretation. Dark dispatch console, live channel ticker, every workflow a traceable route with timestamped legs. Ops leaders read it as a tool I already wish I had.

08 · Cream + stamp red

Case file

Round 3 · voice & narrative
Mood
A patient's path, automated.
A single patient — Jane Doe, CPAP — carries the whole site as a four-chapter story: the fax arrives, the policy applies, the phone rings, the claim is paid. The most narrative of the variants, and the one a clinician is most likely to read end-to-end.

The Record

Mood
A newspaper of record.
Full editorial newspaper. Masthead, three-column front page, lead story with drop cap and pull quote, bulletin ticker, classified-style onboarding, correspondence section. Says serious institution before it says anything else.
